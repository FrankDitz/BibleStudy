# Virtue Course Catholic Review Packet

Use this packet before changing any virtue course from in-review to approved. Completion by the author or by an automated system is not approval. The reviewer should be a qualified Catholic theologian, catechist, cleric, or other person formally designated by the project owner and competent for the material being reviewed.

## 1. Doctrinal fidelity

- [ ] Definitions agree with the Catechism and the cited magisterial sources.
- [ ] Human, cardinal, theological, infused, and acquired virtues are distinguished accurately.
- [ ] Grace and free cooperation are taught together without self-salvation or passivity.
- [ ] Conscience is presented as a judgment that must be followed and formed, not as preference or external control.
- [ ] Moral acts are evaluated through object, intention, and circumstances.
- [ ] No good intention is said to justify an evil means.
- [ ] Objective moral truth is distinguished from personal culpability without pretending to judge the soul.
- [ ] Course completion is never described as holiness, mastery, merit, or state of grace.

## 2. Scripture

- [ ] Every reference exists in the Catholic canon and opens the intended full passage.
- [ ] Introductions and syntheses respect literary and canonical context.
- [ ] Scripture is not used as a slogan, proof-text, or substitute for moral reasoning.
- [ ] Christ remains the center and measure of Christian virtue.
- [ ] Translation text is displayed only through a licensed provider or other authorized source.

## 3. Cases, witnesses, and application

- [ ] Worked cases identify missing facts and avoid confident verdicts about culpability.
- [ ] Analyses distinguish the chosen act, intention, circumstances, effects, duties, and possible repair.
- [ ] Saints and historical persons are represented accurately and linked to a traceable source.
- [ ] Examples do not turn suffering, abuse, illness, disability, or trauma into a spiritual shortcut.
- [ ] Small, ordinary, and demanding practices are safe, concrete, and genuinely connected to the lesson.
- [ ] Practice remains voluntary and routes into the existing Focus Cycle rather than a duplicate tracker.

## 4. Pastoral safety

- [ ] Language avoids shame, spiritual comparison, stereotypes, and diagnosis.
- [ ] Chastity, addiction, trauma, compulsion, mental illness, and scrupulosity receive careful boundaries.
- [ ] The course directs dangerous, abusive, illegal, sacramental, or clinically serious cases to appropriate help.
- [ ] No prompt asks the learner to judge another person's soul.
- [ ] Returning after failure is framed through truth, repair, mercy, and hope—not punishment or lost streaks.
- [ ] Private notes are optional, local, ungraded, and never interpreted by the app.

## 5. Learning quality

- [ ] The preview honestly states purpose, scope, length, sources, and review state.
- [ ] Each lesson has a coherent learning goal and sufficient authored instruction.
- [ ] Terms are defined before they carry argumentative weight.
- [ ] Each case offers guided reasoning after the learner has a chance to think.
- [ ] Assigned passages receive an authored synthesis and transfer question.
- [ ] Self-examination moves from facts and perceived goods through choice, effects, virtue, repair, and grace.
- [ ] Cumulative checks provide explanatory feedback and remain ungraded with unlimited retry.
- [ ] The final lesson gives a clear next step without implying an endpoint to formation.

## 6. Source, rights, and freshness

- [ ] Every doctrinal claim and historical witness is traceable to the displayed source list.
- [ ] Primary Church sources govern doctrine; historical secondary sources are clearly identified.
- [ ] Paraphrases do not reproduce copyrighted commentary or Bible text beyond permission.
- [ ] Source links, locators, and checked dates are current.
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific discipline is identified and dated when it appears.
- [ ] A future review-by date is assigned.

## 7. Product and accessibility acceptance

- [ ] VoiceOver presents headings, questions, options, feedback, and completion state meaningfully.
- [ ] Dynamic Type does not truncate teaching, Scripture context, or response controls.
- [ ] Keyboard navigation and macOS presentation are usable.
- [ ] Status is communicated with text and symbols, not color alone.
- [ ] Scripture routes preserve the complete requested range and return to Virtues.
- [ ] Legacy progress and private notes decode after upgrading.
- [ ] Export and delete behavior includes private virtue reflections without leaking them to analytics, logs, provider requests, or hub events.

## Approval rule

The course may be marked approved only when every blocking item is checked, all requested revisions are incorporated, the reviewed commit and content version match the shipped build, and the qualified reviewer signs the record. Automated tests establish structural quality; they do not establish doctrinal approval.
