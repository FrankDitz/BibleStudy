## Ready-to-send message

**Subject:** Personal native Bible app — permission and technical availability inquiry

Hello STREETLIGHTS team,

I am building a private, non-commercial Bible study app for my own use on my iPhone and Mac. It will not be publicly distributed, sold, or used by an organization. The app is part of a personal Christian self-improvement hub and includes Scripture reading, private reading plans, Catholic learning material, and private reflection prompts.

I value STREETLIGHTS’ English Audio Bible and would like to understand whether you would permit its audio to be played inside this private native app. I will not scrape your website, copy a stream, or include your recordings without written permission and confirmation of the underlying publisher rights.

Could you please tell me:

1. whether owner-only, in-app playback is permitted;
2. whether you offer an API, feed, licensed source URL, SDK, or another approved integration method;
3. whether playback must stream or may use a temporary local file, and whether persistent offline downloads are allowed;
4. whether chapter- or verse-level timing data is available for synchronized highlighting;
5. which English Bible translation and recording rights the permission covers, including whether NLT publisher approval is already included or must be obtained separately;
6. which books are available and whether the recording includes the full Catholic canon and the Catholic portions of Esther and Daniel;
7. the exact attribution, artwork, links, and notices that must appear in the player;
8. whether usage reporting, analytics, FUMS, rate limits, or other technical obligations apply; and
9. how access, cached files, and attribution should be handled if permission is later changed or revoked.

If personal in-app integration is not available, may the app instead provide a clearly labeled link that opens the relevant passage or chapter in an official STREETLIGHTS experience? If so, please share the supported URL or deep-link format and any attribution requirements.

I would be happy to provide screenshots or a short technical outline. I will keep the audio feature disabled unless and until we have written permission and a technically supported source.

Thank you,

`[Owner name]`  
`[Owner email]`

## Response classification

| Response | Engineering action |
| --- | --- |
| Written permission plus supported source | Record exact scope; add a provider adapter spike only after translation rights and attribution are confirmed |
| Link-out permission only | Offer an external action if its URL scheme is stable and the requested attribution is acceptable |
| Permission requires separate NLT/publisher approval | Keep audio disabled until both permissions are documented |
| No response or no permission | Keep STREETLIGHTS out of the app; continue with API.Bible or another licensed provider |
| Public-release-only or commercial terms | Treat as out of current scope; reassess only if distribution changes |

## What Git may record

Git may contain public contact routes, non-secret technical facts, the dates of owner confirmations, and a summary of granted capabilities. It must not contain private correspondence, signatures, account identifiers, contracts, access URLs, credentials, or copies of license documents.
